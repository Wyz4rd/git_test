Privacy Commitment, respecting your privacy

This Privacy Policy is relevant to Jeremy Politi and Obsidian Broking Group.

We understand how important it is to protect your personal information. This document sets out our privacy policy commitment in respect of personal information that you may provide to us.

Our Privacy Policy ensures your right to privacy as we recognise that any personal information we may collect about you will only be used for the purposes indicated in our policy. It is important to us that you are confident that any personal information we collect from you or is received by us, will be treated with appropriate respect ensuring protection of your personal information.

If you are in a country that is a member of the European Economic Area (EEA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 (GDRP) governs the way we collect, use, hold, process and disclose your personal information. Under GDPR, we are a data controller. We make decisions on how and why your personal information is processed.

This Policy also includes our credit reporting policy, that is, it covers additional information on how we manage your personal information collected in connection with a credit application, or a credit facility. We refer to this credit-related information below as credit information.

Obsidian Broking group is committed to complying with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles and any other relevant laws when handling your personal information. A copy of the Australian Privacy Principles may be obtained from the website of The Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at www.oaic.gov.au.

Who is Jeremy Politi and Obsidian Broking Group

Obsidian Broking Group provides a premium, client-first mortgage broking service designed to deliver clarity, confidence and exceptional outcomes for homebuyers, investors and business owners across Australia. The business operates with integrity, transparency and a commitment to simplifying the lending process from start to finish.

Core Services

  • Home Loans Helping first-home buyers, upgraders and refinancers secure competitive lending solutions tailored to their financial goals.
  • Investment Loans Structuring finance for property investors seeking long-term growth, tax efficiency and strategic lending options.
  • Refinancing & Restructuring Reviewing existing loans to reduce repayments, improve loan features or consolidate debt for better financial control.
  • Commercial & Business Finance Supporting business owners with funding for commercial property, equipment, vehicles and business expansion.
  • Self-Employed & Complex Lending Providing specialist guidance for clients with non-standard income, including company directors, contractors and sole traders.

What Obsidian Broking Group Offers

  • Client-First Options Transparent, fair and compliant guidance focused solely on the client’s best interests.
  • Access to a Wide Lender Panel Major banks, second-tier lenders and specialist providers to ensure flexible, competitive solutions.
  • Clear Communication & Support Straightforward explanations, proactive updates and a smooth, stress-free lending experience.
  • Strategic Loan Structuring Tailored recommendations designed to support long-term financial stability and future borrowing capacity.
  • End-to-End Service From initial strategy call to settlement and ongoing annual reviews, clients receive continuous support.
  • Professionalism & Integrity A commitment to compliance, ethical conduct, and delivering outcomes that build trust and long-term relationships.
